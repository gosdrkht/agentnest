Frequently Asked Questions

What Docker images can I deploy?

+

Any Docker image from Docker Hub or any public registry. Ubuntu, Python, Node.js, custom images - anything you can containerize.

Is there a free trial?

+

Yes! Start with our free Starter tier (1 agent, 512MB). Or get a 30-day free trial of Professional (no credit card required).

+

Absolutely. Adjust CPU and memory limits anytime. AgentNest handles auto-scaling, health checks, and crash recovery.

Yes. You own your data. Export logs, metrics, and container configs anytime. No vendor lock-in.
